OPEN METHODOLOGY

How We Calculate This

01

The Live Ticker (Tier 2: Estimated)

The ticking counter is an estimate based on macroeconomic benchmarks. The 20% figure is drawn from the UNODC ("corruption costs developing countries 10–25% of public procurement value") and World Bank governance assessments, which classify the Philippines as high-risk for procurement leakage. The live meter tracks cumulative estimated losses since January 1, 2020 to the present day in real time.

₱5,768,000,000,000 (GAA benchmark) × 0.20 = ₱1,153,600,000,000 / year
÷ 31,536,000 seconds = ~₱36,591 / second
Elapsed since Jan 1, 2020: ~213M+ seconds × ~₱36,591/sec ≈ ₱7.8 Trillion
02

Case Registry (Tier 1: Documented)

Every entry in our case registry is linked to an official document: a COA Notice of Disallowance, a Sandiganbayan decision, a PCGG accomplishment report, or a Senate Blue Ribbon Committee resolution. We do not editorialize guilt. We cite official legal status only.

03

Legal Disclaimer

This site does not declare guilt. All individuals mentioned are cited in connection with official proceedings only. Terms such as "Indicted," "Under Litigation," and "COA Flagged" refer to official legal designations. This site is an educational public-interest accountability tool built under the principles of press freedom.
