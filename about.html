WHY WE EXIST

Public money belongs
to the public.

Korap Tracker tracks where it goes, and where it doesn't.

Back to Tracker

Every peso lost to corruption is a school without a teacher, a patient turned away, a road never built. We built this tracker so those facts stay on the record. Permanently. Clearly. For everyone.

01
To Inform Facts only. Every number links to an official COA report, court filing, or Senate record. Nothing is made up.
02
To Educate ₱67 billion sounds abstract. "67,000 fully equipped classrooms" doesn't. We make the numbers human.
03
For Awareness Scandals vanish from the news in weeks. We keep the record open. Accountability shouldn't have an expiry date.
04
To Criticize is to Improve Asking questions about public money is not disloyalty. It is the job of every citizen in a democracy.
