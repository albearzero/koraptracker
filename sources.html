Data Integrity Commitment

This page is the authoritative record for all figures on Korap Tracker. Each entry includes the exact source document, the specific quote or figure used, methodological notes, and a direct URL. Where figures were found to be incorrect or unverifiable in initial data, corrections are documented below. This tracker is designed to be challenged, and if you find an error, the correction will appear here. Cyberlibel laws under RA 10175 are taken seriously; all amounts reference official proceedings, not editorial opinion.

⚠ Data Corrections Log: Version 1.1 (Oct 2024)

CORRECTED NFA Case: Initial figure of ₱700B was removed; no verifiable COA report supports this specific aggregate total. Replaced with COA-documented ₱5B in rice inventory losses (2019 to 2023) per GMA News citing COA findings.
CORRECTED MWSS Case: Initial figure of ₱1.9B was unverified. Corrected to ₱845.71M, the specific COA-flagged amount for excess Kaliwa Dam collections, per Inquirer.net citing the COA 2022 AAR on MWSS.
CORRECTED DepEd Laptop Case: Year corrected from 2014 to 2021 (Bayanihan 2 Act procurement). The ₱2.4B total contract figure is retained; the Senate overpricing estimate is ₱979M.
CORRECTED Marcos/PCGG Case: Updated from ₱175B to ₱280B to reflect the most recent (2023) PCGG accomplishment data (~₱180B cash + ₱100B assets recovered). Original ₱589B / $10B ill-gotten wealth estimate retained as baseline.
CORRECTED PDAF Source: GR 213847 (Enrile bail case) was removed as a primary citation for Napoles conviction. Replaced with Wikipedia's PDAF scam article (citing Senate, Ombudsman, and Sandiganbayan records) and PCIJ's full Napoles conviction report.
CLARIFIED 20% Ticker Benchmark: Attribution to Ombudsman Morales was removed; no verifiable public statement with that specific figure was found. The 20% figure is now correctly attributed to the World Bank and UNODC international procurement leakage benchmarks, consistently applied to Philippines in ADB country risk assessments. The ticker is explicitly labeled as an ESTIMATE throughout the site.

3. Human Cost Calculator: Unit Cost Basis
DepEd School Building Program: ₱250,000/Classroom Unit Cost
Government / Official
Department of Education School Building Program
Standard DepEd School Building Program unit cost for a single-storey classroom is approximately ₱250,000 (simple construction). Multi-storey and typhoon-resistant variants range higher.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Education_(Philippines)
PhilHealth Benefits Package: ~₱50,000/Hospitalization Average
Government / Official
Philippine Health Insurance Corporation
PhilHealth case rates for standard hospital admissions range from ₱8,000 to ₱120,000+ depending on diagnosis. ₱50,000 is used as a conservative average across mid-severity cases.
https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/benefits/
CHED Tuition Subsidy: ~₱100,000/Scholar/Year
Government / Official
Commission on Higher Education: UniFAST Program
Under RA 10931 (Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act), students in SUCs receive free tuition. CHED-administered scholarship subsidies under UniFAST programs provide ~₱60,000 to ₱100,000 per beneficiary annually for private HEI students.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commission_on_Higher_Education_(Philippines)
